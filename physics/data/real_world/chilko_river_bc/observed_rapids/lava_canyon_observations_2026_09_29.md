## Where the reach sits
- Bidwell Creek mouth is at 51.923 N, 123.818 W (937 m), about 3.5-4.5 km upstream of station 0. BC Whitewater says Bidwell Rapid is about 5 km below the Bidwell Creek put-in and the White Mile is about 3 km after Bidwell. That matches our data: Bidwell at about 690-1000 m, and the White Mile starting at about 3600 m.
- Named sequence, going downstream:
  1. Scouting eddy (river left)
  2. Blind corner
  3. **Bidwell**
  4. **White Kilometer**
  5. "The last canyon"
  6. **White Mile**, which continues past the reach end
- Below the reach come Eagle's Claw, the Lava Canyon basalt box canyon and Landslide Rapid.
- Outfitters call the whole run "Lava Canyon". Kayak and guidebook sources use the name only for the black-basalt slot below the White Mile, which is outside our reach.

## Flow at about 93 m3/s
- Gauge 08MA002 read about 1.51 m in 2015. For comparison: 1.30 m is about 63 m3/s and 1.60 m is about 105 m3/s.
- A long-time local kayaker calls 1.5 m the *prime* level: big waves, and Bidwell is pushy but manageable.
- BC Whitewater counts anything over 70 m3/s as high water. Above about 105 m3/s some features wash out. Mid-July peaks (110-130 m3/s or more) are class IV+/V.
- The 1987 fatal flip happened at 117 m3/s (gauge record). American Whitewater's figure of "10,000 cfs" is wrong.
- Best matching footage: 2014-08-26 at 96 m3/s and August 2017 at 87-97 m3/s.

**What the White Mile looks like.** At about 3600-3650 m the river makes a sharp right turn. Dark, deep water sits on the outside (left) and a bright pour-over sits on the inside (right) of the bend. After that the river runs east as one continuous wave train with no eddies, about 27-32 m wide.

- **Wave size:** sources report waves of "3-4.5 m" or "12-14 ft", but these are probably perceived heights. In video at this flow the crests look about 1.5-2.5 m, with some around 3 m.
- **Holes:** "a couple of holes, easy to avoid".
- **Length:** it continues roughly 1-1.6 km past the end of the reach.
- **Banks:** river left is an open grassy slope facing south. River right is dense conifer forest facing north.

## Banks and vegetation
- **Bidwell:** buff/tan-grey volcanic bluffs, about 15-40 m high, on both sides, with dark basalt boulder talus at the waterline.
- **Talus fans:** grey talus fans reach the river on river right at about 2700 m and about 3600 m.
- **Slopes by aspect:** south-facing slopes are open bunchgrass with scattered Douglas-fir and lodgepole pine. North-facing slopes are dense lodgepole pine, spruce and Douglas-fir down to the water.
- **Other trees:** there are aspen patches, and 2007-era photos show red beetle-killed pines.
- **Water:** clear and turquoise.
