# Hance Rapid (Colorado River, Grand Canyon): observation summary

Companion to `colorado_hance_observations.json`. Researched 2026-09-29. Stations are on the `colorado_hance` centreline, running 0 (E, upstream) to 2512 m (W). Lateral positions are fractions of the wetted width measured from the LEFT bank, facing downstream. Left is the south side and the Red Canyon side.

## Anchors (projected onto the centreline)
- AW Hance POI (head of the rapid): station 678, on the centreline.
- GNIS Hance Rapids label: 790.
- GNIS Red Canyon mouth: 729, 119 m river left. The debris fan comes from the LEFT.
- Michaud's river-right scout trail: 447, 46 m right.
- Mineral Canyon (left): about 2560 m, at the reach end.
- Asbestos Canyon (right): about 2950 m, outside the reach.
- Sockdolager: about 3600 m, outside the reach. It is not in the 2.5 km reach.
- Channel shape: the river bends right (W to NW) at 400-650 m, runs straight NW at 600-800 m (the head of the rapid), then bends LEFT back to W at 800-1000 m (the body of the rapid). Guides say the left turn pushes boats right into the big holes.

## Totals
- Drop: about 30 ft (9.1 m) over "a little over half a mile" (0.8-1.0 km). This probably runs from the head (about 680 m) through Son of Hance (about 1300-1420 m), which matches the orthophoto whitewater at 680-1450 m.
- No published split of the drop between Hance and Son of Hance was found.
- Rating: 8 out of 10. American Whitewater calls it IV+.

Name check: "Duck on a Rock" is a South Rim viewpoint, not a Hance feature. The real in-rapid names are **Duck Pond**, **Emilio's Hole**, **Land of the Giants**, **Rubber Magnet** and **Muffin/Brain/Hamburger Rock**, plus **Son of Hance** below.

## How the features change with flow
- **Below 5k:** large boats cannot get through (Kieffer, 1987). The head is very rocky.
- **About 6k:** the rapid plays like pinball and the Duck Pond disappears. A far-right run opens up at 5-9.5k because the right-side holes weaken; the final central hole still stops boats.
- **About 8k (modelled flow):**
  - The standard line is a downstream ferry from the right, between the pour-overs and left of Emilio's Hole, into the Duck Pond, then left past the three dry rocks.
  - The left side is too rocky.
  - Emilio's Hole is a keeper.
- **10-16k:** the same line works. The top hole still breaks oars at around 16k.
- **18-20k:**
  - Entrance boulders can still pin boats (a raft pinned at 18k).
  - Rocks are covered and holes are bigger.
  - A left run is reportedly possible again at medium to high water.
  - Son of Hance develops large waves.
- **About 80k (1983):** Hance becomes a single V skewed to the right, with big holes under the left arm and large tail waves. It was rated 9-10.

## Banks, terrain and vegetation (details in the JSON)
- **River left:**
  - The Red Canyon boulder fan, with boulders 0.1-2 m across.
  - A large sand dune at the mouth. The New Hance and Tonto trail junction sits at its base.
  - Camp beaches above the rapid and at the foot of the dune below it.
  - Camelthorn and tamarisk choke the shoreline upstream of Red Canyon (NPS).
- **River right:**
  - Red-orange Hakatai Shale (about 135 m thick) cut by the famous grey-black basalt dike at the head of the rapid.
  - The high scout trail.
  - A diabase sill in the Bass Formation with the white talus of the Hance asbestos mine, further downstream.
  - Unverified: springs with riparian vegetation at 76.7R.
- **Downstream:**
  - The Hotauta conglomerate and Bass Formation reach river level near the tail of the rapid.
  - Vishnu Schist and Zoroaster Granite start immediately below: the Upper Granite Gorge, with steep, dark walls and few beaches.
