# Zambezi Batoka Gorge, rapids 1-25 at low water: research summary

Full detail, with a source URL next to every fact, is in `batoka_run_observations_2026_09_30.json` in the same folder. Researched 2026-09-30. The per-rapid stations used in the game are in `batoka_run_observed_rapids.json` (`rapid_stations`).

## How to read this

- **Sides.** Facing downstream, river left is Zambia (L) and river right is Zimbabwe (R).
- **Station.** Stations are metres along the repo production route (`production_corridor/.../route_stationing.json`, 0 at the Boiling Pot).
  - I derived them from Sentinel-2 whitewater on 2025-10-03 (283 m³/s) and 2024-10-08 (203 m³/s).
  - I cross-checked them against tributary confluences in OpenStreetMap, the Taita Falcon Lodge GPS position ("above rapids 16 & 17"), the ERM rapid map, and published rafting km.
  - None of these stations is surveyed.
- **Confidence.** H = high, M = medium, L = low.

## Banks, terrain and vegetation

- **Rock:** Batoka Formation basalt throughout. Black vertical cliffs sit above scree/talus slopes. There is little exposed rock in the pools; the pool-drop rapids form at constrictions and ledges.
- **Gorge depth:**
  - About 100-145 m in the 3rd to 5th Gorges.
  - About 150-180 m around Rapids 8-13; the Songwe Gorge is the deepest of the zig-zag gorges.
  - About 190-235 m from Rapid 14 to 25: 226-230 m at Taita Falcon Lodge, and about 220 m at the Rapid 25 take-out.
  - Rims are about 880-910 m a.s.l. on both sides.
- **Beaches:** White sand backed by black basalt.
  - Known beaches: a camp below Rapid 10 on the left; a lunch beach below a trickling waterfall between Rapids 13 and 16; Bobo Beach (left, Rapid 21); the very large Mukuni Beach (left, Rapid 25).
  - Sand seen in Sentinel-2 at low water: around 24.35 km L, 28.9 km L (the largest), 22.9-23.1 km R, 27.6 km R and 29.2 km L.

- **Vegetation:**
  - The plateau carries mopane woodland. Riparian forest is patchy (Diospyros, Trichilia, Rhus).
  - Screes carry thick mixed woodland (Triplochiton, Commiphora, Entandrophragma, Sterculia) with patches of grass.
  - Near the Falls there is rainforest fed by the spray.
  - In the Aug-Dec imagery the slopes are tan and brown: leafless dry-season woodland, dry grass and pale bare talus. The only green is a thin fringe at the waterline and ribbons along the side ravines.
  - Below the numbered rapids the cliffs give way to golden hills with baobabs.

## Contradictions with the in-game names and order

1. "Between Two Worlds" is **Rapid 2** in every operator source, not Rapid 3. Rapid 3 is unnamed.
2. "The Last Straw" (19) is not attested anywhere. Rapids 19, 20 and 21 are unnamed in every source.
3. Numbering below 21 shifts by one between operators:
   - WBN blog: 22 Morning Shave, 23 Morning Shower.
   - Wild Horizons and the 2019 WBN/ADVNTURR videos: 23 Morning Shave, 24 Morning Shower, 25 Closed Season.
   - victoriafalls-guide: Closed Season is "#26".
4. Other names to add: 11 is also "Creamy White Buttocks"; 12 is also "Three Sisters" or "Three Little Pigs"; 17 is also "The Bitch". "Surprise Surprise" (14) is supported.
5. Two conflicts are unresolved:
   - The ESIA (as paraphrased) has 18 "below Gorges Lodge", but OSM places that lodge at ~21.8 km.
   - The ESIA puts Rapid 23 at ~20 km; Jenkins 2019 puts it at ~26 km.
