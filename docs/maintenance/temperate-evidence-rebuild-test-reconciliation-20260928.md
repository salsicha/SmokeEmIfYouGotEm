The Terminator (2026-09-27) and Lava Canyon (2026-09-28) evidence rebuilds
replaced both 600 m reach-local scenes. The older photoreal, shoreline, corridor
and understory tests still pinned facts of the retired scenes, and 28 of their
hash-locked text artifacts had separately drifted to CRLF.

## Exact LF recovery (no digest changed)

Following [the South Fork precedent](frozen-evidence-line-endings-20260925.md),
each Chilko/Futaleufú/cold-water review artifact whose bytes differed only by
CRLF was rewritten as LF, and only when that reproduced a recorded review
SHA256 exactly. 27 were JSON files whose LFS objects carried CRLF (from
f3b10bbb7): map-load and material-audit receipts, the retired visual-terrain
manifests, centrelines and coordinate maps, the two solver-visualization
manifests, four texture provenance files and one PolyHaven source manifest.
The 28th, `RaftSimEditorChilkoMaterial.cpp`, had checkout conversion and
now has an exact-path `text eol=lf` attribute. No review, digest or assertion
changed.
